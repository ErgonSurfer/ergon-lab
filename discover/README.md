> **Ergon keeps Bitcoin's peer-to-peer proof-of-work architecture, but asks a
> different monetary question: what if issuance responded to the work securing
> the network instead of following a fixed calendar?**

Ergon is a proof-of-work network with its own native currency, XRG, and its own
chain. Its defining protocol idea is proportional reward: the block subsidy is
derived from the work represented by mining difficulty, then adjusted over time
for an assumed trend in hardware efficiency.

That mechanism is implemented. Its wider economic consequences remain a field
of inquiry. Ergon is designed to explore monetary stability; it is not pegged
to another asset and no price is guaranteed.

## How to read this guide

Each chapter makes its epistemic position visible:

| Label | What it promises |
| --- | --- |
| `Explainer` | A source-grounded account of established material |
| `Hypothesis` | A proposition that can be challenged and falsified |
| `Simulation` | A result from declared code, assumptions, and inputs |
| `Observed` | A bounded measurement from a declared environment |
| `Reproduced` | An independently repeated result using reviewed public inputs |
| `Open Question` | A question whose answer is not yet supported |

Protocol behavior comes from reviewed source and tests. Economic interpretation
comes from declared models and evidence. Community essays remain attributed
arguments. A compelling idea is allowed to remain an open question.
